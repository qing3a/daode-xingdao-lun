## What Is This? · 这是什么

The *Tao Te Ching* (道德经, "The Book of the Way and Its Power") is a ~2,500-year-old Chinese classic. This repository presents a new reading of it. It is **not a translation** — it is an **interpretation**: the author rewrites all 80 chapters in a consistent modern vocabulary (survival, development, needs, desires, cooperation, structure, balance), then annotates each line with its structural role.

The goal: make the *Tao Te Ching*'s ancient wisdom *practical and testable* — something you can use to understand organizations, markets, societies, and your own life, not just admire as poetry.

《道德经》是约 2500 年前的中国经典。本仓库不是翻译，而是**逐章再阐释**：作者用一套统一的现代词汇（生存、发展、需求、欲望、协作、结构、均衡）重写了全部 80 章，并给每一句标注了它在结构中的角色，让古老的智慧变得**可实践、可检验**。

## The Essence of the Tao Te Ching · 道德经的本质

Written around the 6th century BCE (the Spring and Autumn period), the *Tao Te Ching* has only ~5,000 Chinese characters in 81 short chapters, yet it is the most translated book in the world after the Bible. It is not a religion — it is a **practical philosophy of how the world works and how to act wisely in it**.

全书仅约五千字、八十一章，却是仅次于《圣经》被翻译最多的书。它不提供信仰，而提供一套**关于世界如何运转、人该如何行动**的实用智慧。六条核心思想：

| # | Idea | 核心理念 | What it means 含义 |
|---|------|----------|--------------------|
| 1 | **The Way (道)** | 道 | The nameless natural order behind all change. It can be followed, but never fully captured in words. 万物变化背后的无名法则，可以被顺应，却无法被言说穷尽。 |
| 2 | **Effortless action (无为)** | 无为 | Act *with* the grain of things, not against it. Force less; align more. 顺着事物的纹理行事，少用蛮力，多作顺应。 |
| 3 | **Softness overcomes hardness (柔弱胜刚强)** | 柔弱胜刚强 | Water is soft, yet it carves stone. Supple, persistent, adaptive — not rigid. 水至柔，却能穿石。柔韧、持续、适应，而非僵硬。 |
| 4 | **Opposites co-arise (有无相生)** | 有无相生 | High and low, gain and loss, full and empty define each other and keep turning into one another. 高与低、得与失、盈与虚互为定义、循环转化。 |
| 5 | **Non-contention (不争)** | 不争 | Don't fight for the top. Serve, stay behind, remain humble — and you end up ahead. 不争首位，服务、谦让、处下，反而走得更远。 |
| 6 | **Simplicity & sufficiency (知足)** | 知足 | Excess breeds trouble. Know when enough is enough; favor long-term survival over short-term grasping. 过犹不及。知止知足，以长远存续胜过短期的攫取。 |

> The common thread: **the Way rewards what sustains life over the long term.** 贯穿始终的主线：**道眷顾那些能长久存续的事物。**

## How This Book Reads It · 本书的读法

Every chapter is rewritten as a **"practice version" (行道版)** — the ancient text recast in modern systems language — and each line is tagged with one of four structural roles:

每一章都被改写成「**行道版**」——用现代系统语言重述原文——并为每句标注四种结构角色之一：

| Tag 标记 | English | 中文 | Role 作用 |
|----------|---------|------|-----------|
| 【现象】 | **Phenomena** | 现象 | What is observed in the world. 世界中可观察到的现象。 |
| 【建模】 | **Modeling** | 建模 | The abstract structure / generative pattern behind the phenomena. 现象背后的抽象结构与生成模式。 |
| 【策略】 | **Strategy** | 策略 | How to act — practical guidance. 如何行动——可执行的指导。 |
| 【评论】 | **Commentary** | 评论 | Reflection on the chapter — often where its "paradox" gets resolved. 对本章的反思——常在此处解开原文的"悖论"。 |

Each chapter is then followed by:

每章之后还配有：

- **注释 · Glossary** — key terms defined one by one (e.g., 道, 德, 有, 无, 得, 施…). 逐条解释本章关键词（道、德、有、无、得、施……）。
- **系统动态图 · System dynamics** — the chapter's core forces in one line (e.g., "生、贵、贱"). 用一句话点出本章的核心力量（如"生、贵、贱"）。
- **说明 / 备注 · Explanation / Notes** — additional reading where needed. 必要的补充说明。

**The conceptual system** (from the foreword): everything that survives can be described as **体 (substance)**, sustained by **力 (force)**, whose combination is **能 (capacity)**; the process that generates them is **得 (gain)**, and its reverse is **失 (loss)** — *"gain and loss is the process of survival and development."* Around this core, the book develops paired concepts: **天地** (heaven & earth), **玄衡** (mystery & balance), **有无** (being & non-being), **得施** (gain & giving), **善与** (goodness & sharing).

**概念系统**（见前言）：凡能存续之物，皆可称为**体**，由**力**维持，体与力之结合为**能**；生成体与力的过程是**得**，反之是**失**——"得与失的过程，就是生存与发展的过程"。围绕这一核心，全书展开成对的概念：天地、玄衡、有无、得施、善与。

## How to Read It · 阅读建议

1. **Read the foreword first.** It gives you the whole conceptual system in three paragraphs — everything else builds on it. 先读前言：三句话给你整套概念系统，其余皆由此展开。
2. **One chapter at a time.** Each chapter is self-contained: read the practice version (行道版), then its glossary (注释). The original *Tao Te Ching* was written for slow reading; so is this. 每次读一章，先读行道版再看注释。原书宜慢读，此书亦然。
3. **Ask "where does this apply?"** This reading is practical by design — for every line, ask: what does this say about organizations, markets, teams, or daily choices? 本书刻意面向实践——每读一句，都可以问：这对组织、市场、团队或日常选择意味着什么？

## About the Source Text · 关于原著

The *Tao Te Ching* (道德经) is attributed to **Laozi (老子)**, traditionally said to have lived in the 6th century BCE. For over two millennia it has shaped Chinese thought, governance, and everyday wisdom, and it continues to speak to modern questions of leadership, sustainability, and balance.

> This work is a personal reinterpretation, not an academic translation. The original text remains the authority; this book is a companion way of reading it. 本书是个人再阐释，非学术翻译。原文仍是权威，本书只是陪伴阅读的一种方式。
